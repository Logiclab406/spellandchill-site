Privacy Policy

Spell and Chill, by Logic Lab 406 LLC. Effective October 19, 2026.

We made Spell and Chill to be played, not to collect your data. This policy explains what the game stores and why.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. Spell and Chill does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and it does not create an account. The game shows ads from Google, which collects some information as described below.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, found words) and settings (sound, vibration, background) are saved on your device so you can keep playing. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Coin packs are bought through Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Apple tells us only that a purchase was made so the game can deliver it.

Advertising

Spell and Chill shows ads through Google AdMob: a small banner, an occasional short ad between levels, and an optional video ad you can choose to watch to double the coins from a level.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and other device identifiers, your IP address and approximate location, information about your device and how ads are shown and used, and crash and performance data. Google uses this as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

On iPhone and iPad, Spell and Chill asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Children

Spell and Chill is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
