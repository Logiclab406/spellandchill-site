Support

Need a hand? Email support@logiclab406.org and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Spell words using the letters on the wooden board: tap letters, or slide your finger across them, then tap Submit. Every word in the grid uses those letters. Extra words that aren't in the grid earn bonus coins.

I'm stuck on a word.

Tap the "…" button at the top for hints: a Letter hint shows one letter, and a Word hint fills in a whole word. Tap an empty square first to choose which word to help with.

Can I make the letters bigger?

Yes. The game follows your iPhone or iPad text size: Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size (or Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Larger Text).

Do I need the internet?

No. Every puzzle plays offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID, then reopen the game. If they still don't appear, email us with the date of purchase.
